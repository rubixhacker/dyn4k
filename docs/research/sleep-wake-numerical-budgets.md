# Numerical-budget readiness for the approved sleep/wake references

Status: research evidence and a proposal for human review; no new numerical acceptance policy is approved here.

Supports [Establish numerical-budget evidence for the approved sleep/wake references](https://github.com/rubixhacker/dyn4k/issues/24) and [Define numerical and migration acceptance](https://github.com/rubixhacker/dyn4k/issues/7).

## Scope and result

The three exact run1 references at 30, 60 and 120 Hz are already [approved](https://github.com/rubixhacker/dyn4k/issues/7#issuecomment-6160940265). Their selection is not reopened. The frozen selection is [manifest revision `4674e089d6f968bf752741f2be85cf2048962552`](https://github.com/rubixhacker/dyn4k/blob/4674e089d6f968bf752741f2be85cf2048962552/docs/research/initial-reference-review-manifest.json), SHA-256 `b42174c7523bea9687e4ab015a215b4e652c2446df73d2f41e836e8a165c1208`; its historical `PENDING_HUMAN_APPROVAL` fields predate the linked approval.

**Finding:** a small, source-justified package of exact numerical consistency checks is ready to propose: fixed floor state, zero applied-force channels, sleeping zero velocities, within-run redundant representations, immediate impulse behavior, and timestep metadata. These do not bound the dynamic body's contact trajectory or its contact/TOI callback geometry and impulses. Those channels require further derivation and an explicit engineering error-budget decision. No nonzero parity or physical limit is inferred from the largest recorded error.

**Proposal:** adopt the narrow exact package below, retain exact discrete sleep/wake and callback matching, and keep numerical acceptance open for the listed dynamic families. Every proposed exact check is scoped to these inputs and to its stated sampling phase. All checks must apply to every in-scope target; there is no platform-specific relaxation.

## Recorded-channel inventory and comparison meaning

The observation manifest enumerates numeric JSON paths and counts from all three selected streams. A numeric JSON value is not automatically a quantity to compare with a tolerance: step indices, order and feature indices are exact discrete identifiers. Conversely, successful ordering or sleep-state checks say nothing about unchecked numeric payloads.

| Recorded family | Meaning and proposed treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Input `dt`, scale, origin, gravity, settings, solver/detector parameters; body/fixture properties, local shape vertices/normals, density, friction/restitution and mass data | Fixture/configuration identity. Literal inputs and discrete values are exact. Derived quantities such as shape radius, inertia, detector epsilons and broadphase perimeter ratio need their own construction formula/runtime analysis before general exact cross-platform comparison; retaining frozen input bytes is not proof that all derived getters have zero error. |
| Body `transform` and `previousTransform`: cosine, sine, translation scalars/vector, six matrix entries, angle | Matrix and translation aliases have exact within-run consistency checks. Dynamic rotation/translation parity remains unbounded. `getRotationAngle` uses signed `acos(cost)`; it is not `atan2(sin,cos)`. Previous transform is a temporal copy requiring the proper phase, not a second independent solution. [Transform][transform] [Previous transform][previous] |
| `worldCenter`, `fixedOriginPosition` | Both local mass centers and configured origin are zero. For these fixtures they coincide numerically with current transform translation; this is a redundant-representation check within each run. It gives no bound on the translation's deviation from the reference. [Body center][center] [Transform][transform] |
| Linear/angular velocities; current/accumulated force and torque | Sleeping velocities and the no-applied-force channels admit the exact checks below. Awake contact velocities remain subject to missing parity budgets. Gravity is integrated separately from the applied-force accumulator, and contact impulses modify velocity; zero `force` does not mean zero acceleration or zero contact force. [Force integration][integration] [Impulse solver][friction] |
| `rotationIncrementSum` | Recorder-derived accumulated wrapped angle increments. It depends on consecutive signed-`acos` outputs followed by `sin`, `cos`, `atan2` and summation. It is not just the integration of angular velocity. Dynamic position correction also rotates transforms. Its nonzero parity budget remains unresolved. [Recorder][recorder] [Position correction][position] |
| Immediate before/after-wake snapshots, including additional properties and fixtures | Same numeric families as the initial state plus action-time trajectory values. Only the specified velocity/sleep changes and otherwise unchanged snapshot content are proposed exact; the pre-action pose itself is not required to equal the baseline with zero error. |
| StepListener `TimeStep` fields | Current/previous timestep, their inverses and ratio. Exact values can be checked for these three fixed binary64 timesteps; the initial previous timestep is the default `1/60`, not necessarily the run timestep. [Timestep][timestep] |
| Collision/ContactListener body velocities and angular velocities | Instantaneous callback-phase dynamic values; cannot substitute the end-of-step state. Floor velocities remain zero. Dynamic values need their own parity budgets. [World callback phases][previous] |
| Penetration and manifold normals; penetration depth; manifold points/depths | Numeric collision geometry. Normal, sign and point ordering belong to a specific callback stage/body order; preserve that association. No physical penetration ceiling or vector/point parity limit is justified yet. [Contact update][contact] |
| ContactConstraint normal/tangent, friction, restitution, restitution velocity, tangent speed | Some are configuration-derived, some geometry-derived; their initialization/update phase matters. Early callbacks expose default friction 0.2 before the fixture mix becomes 0.5. Do not demand that every callback already contain final mixed coefficients or populated geometry. [Contact construction/update][contact] |
| Individual contacts in constraints and callback arguments: point, depth, normal/tangential impulses, manifold feature indices, solved/ignored flags | Point/depth/impulse quantities need numeric budgets; indices/flags and contact identity are exact discrete data. All finite numeric leaves must be inspected. Missing, extra, nonfinite or unclassified fields must fail a future acceptance comparator rather than silently bypass numeric coverage. Repeated aliases inherit their owner's comparison result only where an exact same-run consistency check establishes the relationship.

## Independently justified exact checks proposed for approval

Here, **exact** means equality of finite numeric values, treating `+0` and `-0` as the same numeric zero; it does not impose a new bitwise serialization requirement. Frozen artifact hashes remain byte-exact identity checks. These are proposals for acceptance, with baseline observations reported separately by the audit.

1. **Fixed floor:** `b0` retains center/translation `(0,-0.25)`, identity rotation and zero linear/angular velocity through every state and immediate action snapshot; its recorded force/torque channels are zero. Its initial previous transform equals current because adding a body copies the transform; later previous transforms inherit that same fixed transform. Infinite mass skips velocity integration; static state skips position integration, and contact corrections multiply its inverse mass/inertia by zero. Do not require a constant floor `atRest` flag: island wake/sleep processing can change that discrete flag. [Add body][addbody] [Integration][integration] [Position correction][position] [Island selection][graph]
2. **Applied-force channels:** current force, accumulated force, current torque and accumulated torque are zero for both bodies in all recorded states and action snapshots. The fixture never queues forces/torques; the constructor starts empty/zero and accumulation resets the current values before adding queued values. Gravity and impulses use different paths. `setAtRest(true)` clears queues and zeros velocities but does **not** itself clear the current force and torque; that part of this invariant relies on this exact fixture. [Body initialization][initialization] [Force accumulation][accumulate] [Sleep setter][sleep]
3. **Sleeping dynamic velocity:** every end-of-step or immediate pre-action snapshot with `b1.atRest=true` has `(vx,vy,omega)=(0,0,0)`. This follows from the sleep setter, with the pinned passive fixture applying no later velocity mutation except its explicitly recorded wake action. Repeated sleeping poses should be unchanged within a run over the uninterrupted sleeping intervals; compare the settled pose to that run's own entry pose, not with zero error to the baseline settled pose. [Sleep setter][sleep] [Island selection][graph]
4. **Immediate center impulse:** the sleeping pre-action velocity is zero; post-action velocity is `(1,0)` and angular velocity is zero, and `atRest` becomes false immediately. The 1 m square with density 1 has mass 1 and inverse mass 1; impulse application computes `v += invMass*impulse`. Passing its freshly obtained world center yields a zero lever arm and hence zero angular increment. Current/previous pose, forces, fixture properties and recorder angle accumulator do not change during this call. This checks the immediate action boundary, not the next physics step after friction/gravity act. [Rectangle mass][mass] [Impulse application][impulse]
5. **Same-run aliases and temporal copies:** translation scalar/vector/matrix representations agree; the matrix entries are `[c,-s,x,s,c,y]`; with zero local center and origin the recorded world/fixed-origin center agrees with translation. Initial previous transform equals initial current transform, and an end-of-step previous transform is the pre-solve transform copied from the preceding state in this fixture. Action snapshots must not advance that copy. These exact relationships propagate an owner's eventual budget without demanding that owner's dynamic values match the baseline exactly. Exclude recomputing an angle with the auditor's math library from this exact alias package. [Transform][transform] [Body center][center] [Add body][addbody] [Step copy][previous]
6. **StepListener timestep metadata:** current timestep is the pinned `1/Hz`; inverse is `Hz`. At step 1, previous timestep/inverse are `1/60` and `60`, and ratios for 30/60/120 Hz are `2`, `1`, `0.5`. From step 2, previous equals current and ratio is `1`. The source initializes the world timestep from default settings, then updates fields before stepping. These exact finite values are specific to the three selected timesteps. [Default timestep][settings] [Timestep initialization][world-init] [Timestep update][timestep]

These checks can expose incorrect wiring, missed sleep zeroing, accidental drift while asleep or wrong immediate impulse behavior. They cannot establish that the dynamic body's awake trajectory, its final resting position, contact geometry, or impulse history is acceptable.

## Discrete acceptance remains separate

The selected reference's per-step `atRest` values, first sleep step, action step, first return-to-sleep step, callback signatures/order and contact identities are exact discrete obligations. A tolerance for position or velocity must not permit a sleep transition one step early or late. Driver action identity and immediate before/action/after ordering also remain exact. Approval of this report's proposed numerical package would not approve fuzzy event matching.

There is a discontinuity behind this distinction. The body sleep timer resets when linear speed squared or absolute angular speed exceeds the configured thresholds; otherwise the timer increments. The island sleeps only after the minimum timer reaches its threshold **and** its position constraints report solved. End-of-step sleeping velocities have already been overwritten with zero. The frozen stream therefore cannot reconstruct the actual pre-zeroing velocity margins, private sleep timer or every internal position-solver branch from sleeping end states alone. [Sleep timer][sleep-timer] [Island decision][island]

The settings `0.01 m/s`, `2 degrees/s`, `0.5 s` and `0.005 m` are algorithm inputs. They do not independently establish permissible reference-versus-port error or an unconditional physical output bound. In particular, the contact solver's return checks the minimum estimated separation observed during its position pass against `-3*linearTolerance`; the island has a finite iteration budget. This is not a proof that every recorded manifold depth or final geometric penetration is at most 5 mm or 15 mm. [Settings][settings] [Position solver][position] [Island iterations][island]

The pinned upstream tests corroborate the narrow method contracts: [impulse tests](https://github.com/dyn4j/dyn4j/blob/058bf6d982a0fb89b54050f929f6ea9dae53b714/src/test/java/org/dyn4j/dynamics/AbstractPhysicsBodyTest.java#L264-L308) check simple immediate velocity changes; [sleep-timer tests](https://github.com/dyn4j/dyn4j/blob/058bf6d982a0fb89b54050f929f6ea9dae53b714/src/test/java/org/dyn4j/dynamics/AbstractPhysicsBodyTest.java#L1003-L1047) check increments and resets; [sleep-setter tests](https://github.com/dyn4j/dyn4j/blob/058bf6d982a0fb89b54050f929f6ea9dae53b714/src/test/java/org/dyn4j/dynamics/AbstractPhysicsBodyTest.java#L1284-L1301) explicitly retain nonzero current force/torque while clearing queues and velocities. These unit cases supply no numerical budget for a 600-second contact trajectory.

## Missing budgets and the evidence needed to derive them

| Unresolved gate | Necessary additional derivation or decision |
| --- | --- |
| Awake center/translation and velocity parity, including the eventual resting pose | Derive a bound for the actual finite-step gravity/contact/friction/CCD computation with its six velocity and two position iterations, warm-start history and branch conditions. A sensitivity/roundoff propagation model needs stable contact-feature/active-set assumptions and explicit input/runtime assumptions. Alternatively, the user must select an independently motivated engineering error budget in meters and m/s for this use case, then qualify it against all target implementations without enlarging it to fit observations. |
| Dynamic transform sine/cosine, angle, previous transform and accumulated angle | Extend the rotation analysis to solver position corrections and TOI interpolation as well as ordinary velocity integration. Bound normalization and trig error, signed-`acos` sensitivity near `cost=1`, wrapped-increment computation and summation; qualify the target math/runtime path. Previously approved free-motion rotation limits do not cover this changed operation graph. Previous-transform aliases may inherit the resulting owner budget after temporal consistency passes. |
| Penetration/manifold/contact points, depths and normal/tangent vectors | Derive geometry error in the callback's actual coordinate frame and stage, including shape transforms, GJK/EPA and clipping, feature-selection margins, normalization and warm-start association. Physical acceptance additionally needs an independently chosen penetration/slip budget and a defined geometric observable; center height alone is not penetration for a rotating square. Use transformed vertices/contact geometry, and state the callback or end-state phase measured. |
| Normal/tangential impulses and callback velocities | Bound effective-mass calculation, warm-start rescaling, sequential friction/normal updates, block-solver branch selection, finite iterations and error accumulation. Specify impulse units and component/vector metric. Friction clamps the tangent impulse using the normal impulse available before that iteration's normal update; this alone does not prove a blanket final-callback `abs(jt) <= mu*jn` gate. [Impulse solver][friction] |
| TOI fraction and separation payloads | Derive error from the actual conservative-advancement/distance path, its iteration and termination conditions, and interpolation, including near-zero separation. Define distinct fraction, distance, point and direction metrics. Configuration epsilon is not automatically a final-output error guarantee. |
| Exact sleep/event timing under nonzero numerical budgets | Add separately authorized diagnostic instrumentation recording the pre-zeroing velocities, private sleep timer, contact position-solver success and threshold/feature/TOI decision margins on the exact selected execution path. Prove or qualify that the chosen nonzero error budgets cannot cross those branch boundaries. Existing exact discrete traces still define the required outcome, but do not establish robustness margins. |
| Derived configuration getters and library metadata | Classify quantities into literal controls, discrete structure, algebraic derivations and runtime-math derivations; trace each derived numeric getter to its owning formula and test its target implementation. Do not infer a zero budget from unchanged values within a Java run. |

**Inference:** physical energy conservation is not an appropriate gate for this setup. The dynamic body is under gravity, receives an external impulse, collides with zero restitution and experiences friction. Any energy accounting proposal must include external work and dissipation plus the numerical method's behavior; it cannot import the nominal isolated free-motion energy invariant. The source explicitly applies gravity, normal impulses, friction impulses and positional corrections. [Integration][integration] [Friction][friction] [Position solver][position]

**Proposal for the next human checkpoint:** approve only the exact package above as additional checks for the three accepted references, while keeping dynamic contact/TOI numerical budgets and physical acceptance open. Follow-up research should establish the branch-margin instrumentation requirements and a defensible contact/rotation error model; any later engineering budget requires explicit user approval. No target runtime, Kotlin port comparison, full acceptance comparator or release qualification is executed or passed by this baseline-only audit.
